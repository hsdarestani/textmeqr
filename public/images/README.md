# TextMe cinematic assets

Original photorealistic images generated for this project using the built-in ImageGen tool. These are generated automotive advertising images, not photographs of an actual branded vehicle. No external stock service is requested at runtime. WebP versions are self-hosted; mobile variants are 1000 × 562.

## night-drive.webp
Prompt: Cinematic photoreal automotive advertising photograph, wide 16:9. Black unbranded luxury sports coupe parked at night in a huge dark brutalist concrete garage, low front three-quarter angle. Vehicle occupies right two thirds, left third deep dark negative space for typography. Wet polished black concrete, electric cobalt blue contour lighting, deep violet shadows, narrow crimson red distant wall light. White LED headlights, atmospheric haze, realistic tires and glossy bodywork, anamorphic lens bloom. No text, no logos, no readable license plate, no UI or QR.

## signal-detail.webp
Prompt: Photoreal premium automotive editorial photograph, landscape 16:9. Extreme close-up of black luxury coupe rear quarter, flowing body panel, thin glowing red taillight, rain droplets on polished black paint and glass, electric blue and violet reflections. Night garage with bokeh, shallow depth of field, dark left area, rich black shadows, crimson red against blue. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI.

The website animates these stills with CSS camera movement, a scroll-driven three-chapter scene, photographic crossfades, staggered typography and IntersectionObserver entrances. Motion can be paused and follows prefers-reduced-motion. No video file or video generation is implied.
